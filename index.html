Frequently Asked Questions

Helpful answers regarding appointments, pain management, and treatment duration.

Are treatments painful at Dental Lounge? expand_more

We focus on gentle technique, pre-numbing gels, and precise local anesthesia to ensure every procedure—from standard cleanings to root canals and veneers—is entirely comfortable.

Do you accept dental insurance or installment plans? expand_more

Yes, we provide itemized medical invoices and documentation for insurance reimbursement. For multi-phase restorative care or smile redesigns, treatments can be planned in manageable steps.

What sets this practice apart? expand_more

Appointments are structured to avoid rushed consultations or crowded waiting rooms. Dr. Huma Babar personally attends to every patient with modern 3D optical diagnostics and unhurried clinical care.
